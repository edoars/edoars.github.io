Welcome! I am a Post-Quantum Cryptography Researcher at the European Commission's [Joint Research Centre](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu), in the Cybersecurity and Digital Technologies unit.

<!-- more -->

My research interests are mainly in post-quantum cryptography, in particular signatures with enhanced capabilities, cryptographic group actions, zero-knowledge proofs and Sigma protocols, and the design and security analysis of cryptographic protocols. I also contributed to the NIST PQC submission [CROSS](https://cross-crypto.com).

From 2020 to 2026, I was a cryptographer at [Telsy](https://telsy.com), where I was part of the Research Group.

From 2020 to 2024, I was a PhD student in Mathematics in the [CrypTO Group](https://crypto.polito.it/) (Cryptography and Number Theory Group) at [Polytechnic of Turin](https://polito.it).

Previously, I received a MSc degree in Mathematics from [University of Trento](https://unitn.it) in 2020, and a BSc degree in Mathematics from [Roma Tre University](https://uniroma3.it) in 2017.
